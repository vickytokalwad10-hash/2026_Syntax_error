      How it works

      From raw mandi data to a decision a farmer can act on.

      1. Market data
      2. ↓
      3. Supply + demand + weather
      4. ↓
      5. Price forecast
      6. ↓
      7. SELL / WAIT recommendation

        Production version will connect verified agricultural datasets and machine-learning models. This prototype uses simulated data and a simple rule-based calculation.

        Our vision

        Give farmers access to the kind of market intelligence normally available only to large traders.

        A farmer's hardest question is not what the price is today — it is what the price will be when the crop reaches the mandi. AgriPulse turns scattered market signals into one clear call: sell now, or wait.
